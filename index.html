A LITTLE MORE CONTEXT

Play the shot
you actually have.

We test target lines across the hole, scatter simulated shots around each one, and estimate the strokes it takes to finish from where they land.

This is a planning aid, not a promise. The model uses a simplified distance-to-hole estimate and fixed penalties for rough and hazards. It doesn’t account for wind, elevation, rollout, or obstacles in the flight path.
